I was always thinking that it will be better if every Indian farmer is able to access internet and get information about whatever he needs, to improve his life, just like i do. Fortunately some others had already thought about it very early in 2003 (it seems i’m very bad in keeping up with all the innovations around me) in IIT Bombay where the Developmental Informatics Lab (DIL) launched *“[aAQUA](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/index "aAQUA - Almost All Questions Answered") “* – a web portal for agriculturists[^1].

## What is aAQUA?

For those who wonder why this name – it’s the abbreviation of “Almost All Questions Answered”. This web portal is a forum for agriculturists where farmers pose their questions either themselves or with the help of a kiosk operator and Experts from various KVKs[^2] will answer to the questions related to their field of expertise within a short period.

Here is an old example post of a [Query Regarding Silk Prices](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/viewthread?thread=520 "Answers by Experts for a question regarding silk prices") where a user wanted to know about the markets for silk cocoons. The Expert replied to the post with information about the auctions held in the respective State and also had attached details about related markets and the dates they will be open. The expert added another post describing where payments were to be made and that the govt. provides official transport to the auctions sites with the fuel charges to be borne by the buyer.

Even other farmers can answer the questions if they have helpful information which will also be reviewed by the Experts as in [this post](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/viewthread?thread=10466 "Query on Sheep farming"). The presence of Experts in every posts ensures that the farmers get correct answers thus making the forum more reliable.

## History of aAQUA

Initially aAQUA was tested in Pabal village of Pune District. Then Kiosks were setup in some of Pune villages followed by training for kiosk operators who were involved in collecting questions from farmers of respective villages and to post them in the web portal. The questions were then answered by Experts from KVKs<sup>&#91;<a title="See in list of references" href="#ref2">2</a>&#93;</sup> immediately. This model is interesting because there are two beneficiaries – the farmers, who get valuable clarifications for their doubts from experts and the kiosk operators, who can earn from other revenue streams like STD booths, Photography, Computer Education, Games, DTP, Data Entry, Internet Cafe, etc, depending on their entrepreneurial abilities. Various campaigns where then arranged to spread awareness among farmers and kiosks and the number of farmers using the application increased.

Till 2006 the whole aAQUA project was managed by DIL of IIT Bombay which then realized the need for incubating a company to manage and improve aAQUA further. So in September 2006, [Agrocom Software Technologies Pvt. Ltd.](http://www.agrocom.co.in/ "Agrocom") was started and the content usage and commercialization rights were transferred from IIT Bombay to Agrocom. Apart from aAQUA Agrocom also released products like paid mobile subscriptions for farmers for weather, paid crop disease forecasts, etc,.[^3] in subsequent years.

## aAQUA Usage by various people[^4]

Farmers use aAQUA to:

- Explore new crops for [multi-cropping](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-cropping "Multiple Cropping - Wikipedia").
- Learn how to start a dairy/poultry/sericulture(silk farming)/bee-keeping etc,. for additional income.
- Find new markets for produce.
- To address pests/diseases on crop/livestock.

Traders and Consultants use it to:

- Influence target segment and promote product/service.

Community use it to:

- Compare competitor products/services.
- Query land record, loan or finance.
- Explore emerging farm operations such as bio-diesel, ethanol, biomass reuse, vermi-composing, etc,.
- Learn about organic methods and inputs.

[ICAR](http://www.icar.org.in/ "Indian Council of Agricultural Research ") & [FAO](http://www.fao.org/ "Food &amp; Agriculture Organization of UN") Scientists use it to:

- Tap into farmer conversations and various prevailing issues.

## Usage Statistics of aAQUA

As of 2011, there were 21984 registered users from 517 districts in aAQUA web portal. There were also 4156 new registrations from 385 districts and 4678 active logins in 2011 alone. In the same year 4161 posts were made by 795 users from 189 districts. And there are 79 Experts answering these questions actively[^5]. For more State wise statistics for numbers of users and questions visit this [link](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/userstatistics "User Statistics - aAQUA").

## Important Features in aAQUA

- Web-Keyboards(in Hindi/Marathi, Telugu, Kannada) for questioning/answering.
- Video tutorials for site usage (Visit [agrocomstpl](https://www.youtube.com/user/agrocomstpl "aAQUA Usage Tutorials")).
- [Popular Keyword Cloud](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/tagcloud "Tag Cloud for Popular Keywords") in English and Marathi.
- Free crop tips on Mobile.
- SMS Question (SMS Format: **agrocom post < question >** Send To: **56767666** or **09848356765** ).
- and [more](http://aaqua.persistent.co.in/aaqua/forum/newfeature "New Features in aAQUA").

Still some languages needed to be supported in the portal, so that it can be used with ease by more farmers.
