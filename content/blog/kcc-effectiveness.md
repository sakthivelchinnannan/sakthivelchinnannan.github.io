I grew up in an agricultural family so I know the difficulties in farming, like coping with lack of water supply for irrigation, lack of knowledge about advanced techniques, lack of cooperation between neighbors, lack of labor nowadays, lack of proper government intervention for various support and most importantly **lack of interest among farmers**.

I work in the software field (as most of the people will call it but I work in Enterprise services industry to be exact) where I realized the importance of following a process for doing something successfully (like following waterfall or agile development models for developing a software), most importantly opting for proven techniques to ensure time saving and success, and many more good practices. So I wondered why isn’t any one think about such things in agriculture.

Enough about myself and my silly doubts. Lets see about a facility initiated by the Ministry of Agriculture in 2004 which was named “**Kisan Call Center**” ([KCC](https://www.manage.gov.in/kcc/kcc.asp)). I heard about it in my school days but failed to notice that no one in my village was using it’s services. But now while thinking (i’m getting more free times!) about the reasons why most of the farmers fail in gaining profit from agriculture i’m getting [more points](http://tejas-iimb.org/interviews/12.php "Indian Agriculture - Issues &amp; Reforms") as answer. One of them was **lack of proper access to information** required by the farmers.

After searching in Google for the effectiveness of [Kisan Call Center](https://www.manage.gov.in/kcc/kcc.asp), one of the search results gave me an impressive information that [84% of the farmers](http://pib.nic.in/newsite/erelease.aspx?relid=83116 "Study conducted by Administrative Staff College of India, Hyderabad (ASCI)") (who used KCC’s service) expressed overall satisfaction regarding the advice provided. And this information was given by Shri Harish Rawat, Minister of State for Agriculture and Food Processing Industries in written reply to a question in the Rajya Sabha on 04-May-2012 (specified in the [“Press Information Bureau” link](http://pib.nic.in/newsite/erelease.aspx?relid=83116)). It also specifies that the KCC service is allotted to a new Service Provider (IFFCO Kisan Sanchar Limited) who promised for many more new features like

1. Upscaling the knowledge of Call Centre Agents (CCA) by way of providing latest versions of guide books and booklets issued by the State Agricultural Department or the Agricultural Universities.
2. Voice mail system for recording farmer’s queries when KCC is closed and when all lines busy, with provision for call back to the caller.
3. SMS to caller farmers providing a gist of advisories given to them on phone., etc,.

So it seems we have an effective system to back farmers for their information requirements. But my doubts are, do every Indian farmer (from towns to deep rural villages) know about this KCC facility where [people in 3 levels are waiting to answer](http://www.manage.gov.in/kcc/infra.htm "KCC Infrastructure - 3 Levels of Help") every farmers’ question in every state? Even if they knew about it, **are they ready and (most importantly) capable**, to think about their current ways of farming, to know exactly what to ask from the KCC and to take action to improve their ways of farming?
